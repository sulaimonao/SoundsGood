SoundsGood Personal Edition Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

SoundsGood Personal Edition is provided by Akeem Oluwadamilare Sulaimon. Privacy and support contact: thedream.akeems@gmail.com. This policy covers Personal Edition and its optional use of the SoundsGood Mac companion. SoundsGood Full has a separate privacy policy.

Music and records on your phone

The app reads audio you allow through Android's media permission or file picker. It uses titles, artists, albums, genres, artwork references, duration, file references and integrity hashes to organize and play music. Its private database stores listening events, play and skip counts, listening time, your mood tags, playlist rules, synced playlists and their order, and playback queue and position. These records support local playback and personalized organization. They are not sent to the developer by the app.

Local sharing and reports

Optional sync connects to a Mac you pair on the same local Wi-Fi network. The phone receives selected music, library metadata and playlist information. Connections use encrypted TLS with the certificate fingerprint supplied in the pairing code. Pairing credentials stay in session memory; disconnect or stop sharing to end the pairing. Keep pairing codes private.

After eligible successful or failed syncs, the phone automatically attempts to send a result report to that paired Mac. It contains opaque library, track, playlist and report IDs, revision, counts, bytes, integrity results and fixed outcome/reason codes. It does not include song titles, file paths or pairing credentials. The latest report is retained privately on the phone and can be explicitly saved to a destination you choose through Android’s document picker. This transfer is between your devices, not a developer telemetry service.

The Mac's optional Desktop report is enabled by default and can contain song titles, artists, timestamps, IDs, transfer results and skipped items. Turn off “Save a sync report on Desktop” before sharing if you do not want those files. Desktop synchronization, including iCloud when enabled, can copy them elsewhere independently of SoundsGood.

Shared folders, file providers and backups

A chosen Music folder stores audio and recovery records under “SoundsGood Library”. Records contain track and library metadata, playlist names and order, and integrity hashes. They remain after uninstall so the library can be restored; apps with access to that folder may also read them. Transfer-folder imports similarly read music and metadata you select.

Automatic Android backup and device transfer of private app data are disabled. This does not cover original music, shared Music folders, exported reports or Mac copies. The individual file picker may expose a cloud-backed provider; that provider and any folder synchronization you enable have their own privacy and retention practices.

Permissions and other services

Audio-library access reads music you permit. Network access and network-state access support local Wi-Fi sync. Foreground-service, notification and wake-lock permissions support playback. Personal sync itself requires the app to stay open. The app has no developer account, advertising, analytics-upload or automatic crash-upload service. It does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts access. Operating-system and file-provider services operate under their own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

Private library and listening records remain until removed in the app where controls exist, or until you clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall. The latest private sync report replaces the previous report. Clear storage is a full reset of private app data; it does not delete your original music or shared/exported copies. Delete shared audio and recovery records, saved reports and Mac copies separately with your file manager. Revoke permissions in Android Settings when you no longer want access. There is no SoundsGood account to delete, and the developer cannot remotely erase files on your devices.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your address and what you choose to send, and uses it to respond and troubleshoot. Send only necessary information. Correspondence is retained only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations; email the contact above to request access, correction or deletion. Do not send music, passwords or pairing codes.

These documentation pages add no analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party scripts. GitHub hosts the published pages and may process technical visit information under its privacy statement. This policy's date will change when the policy is updated.
