Tell us whether you use SoundsGood Personal Edition or SoundsGood Full, your app version, your Android or macOS version, and the steps that produced the problem. Include the exact error wording when possible. A short description is usually a better starting point than a complete library export or device log.

Protect your information

Do not post personal music, journal text, pairing links, credentials, signing keys, private file paths or unreviewed diagnostic files in a public issue. Android result reports use identifiers and fixed status codes, while Mac Desktop reports can contain song titles and artists. Full's backup files are unencrypted and can contain private journals. Review any file before sharing it. Ask privately by email before sending potentially sensitive evidence.

Music is missing

  • Check audio permission in Android settings and confirm the music is present and readable.
  • For a selected folder, confirm that its access grant is still valid. A moved folder, disconnected drive or changed document provider may need to be selected again.
  • Refresh or rescan from the relevant library/source control. Do not clear app storage as a first troubleshooting step: it removes private library information and settings.
  • A successful copy does not guarantee decoder support. Protected, unavailable or unsupported files may be skipped, and an unusual container can fail even when its filename has a supported extension.

Mac synchronization

The Mac download is forthcoming. No public installer is available. If macOS blocks a copy, do not override Gatekeeper.

See the Mac companion guide for current availability and setup. The Mac creates the pairing code after you choose music and start sharing. Paste that code into the intended Android edition's Local Sync screen. If multiple editions are installed, an operating-system link chooser may appear; choose the edition you mean to use.

Use the same trusted local network. Guest Wi-Fi, client isolation, a VPN, firewall restrictions or the wrong Mac network address can prevent a connection. Confirm that the Mac is still sharing and use its current code. Do not post the code in a public issue or expose the companion to the public internet.

Personal Edition: keep its sync screen/app in the foreground during transfers. It does not include Full's foreground-service background-sync behavior. Full: user-started sync uses a foreground service with progress and cancellation controls and is designed to continue while you use other screens or turn the display off. Device power settings and lost connectivity can still interrupt a transfer. Check the final result before assuming it completed.

Choose separate managed destination folders for separate editions. App-private databases are separate, but both apps can access a shared folder if you grant them access. Avoid having both editions write to the same managed destination.

Backups and deletion

Full can export library customizations as unencrypted JSON; this does not back up the audio files. Keep your own audio backups. A system document provider or Desktop synchronization service may copy exported files to the cloud according to its settings.

Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes private app data, but does not necessarily remove music and exports in folders you selected. Manage those copies separately. Privacy questions and deletion requests concerning information sent to the publisher can be emailed to the contact above.
